The brief

Turn a factory service into a brand decision.

BlueDots Printery prints custom bottles for brands in bulk: skincare, haircare, perfume and more. Their buyers are business owners, and the main goal was clear: get a steady flow of leads through Meta ads.

Instead of advertising “we print bottles”, we positioned BlueDots as the expert on packaging that sells. Every post answers a question a brand owner is already asking, so by the time they fill in the form, they're already sold on the idea.

The funnel
01 · Awareness High performing

Reach brand owners cheaply

The awareness campaign put BlueDots in front of 622,584 people at just Rs 21.50 per 1,000.

02 · Consideration

Send them to the profile

A profile-visit campaign brought interested buyers to the page to see more work. The page logged 16K visits over the period.

03 · Conversion High performing

Capture the lead

Instant lead forms turned that attention into 750+ bulk-order enquiries at around Rs 40 each.
