The brief

Make every post feel like a luxury lookbook.

Amna Naeem Footwear sells designer flats and heels across Pakistan, with orders coming in through Instagram DMs and WhatsApp on cash on delivery. That makes the Instagram grid the brand's shop window: it has to build trust and make people want to message.

[THE CHALLENGE: what wasn't working before Zela? e.g. inconsistent posting, product photos that didn't match the price point, low engagement.]

01

Social media handling

Ran the Instagram account day to day: planning content around each collection and keeping highlights like Sale, Exhibitions and How to Order working for new customers.

02

Graphic design

Designed collection launches with a quiet, editorial look: The Signature Line, The Luxe Slingback and Luxe Mule collections, Golden Elegance and WearUp.

03

Posting

Published on a steady schedule, pairing product close-ups with styled on-foot shots so the grid reads as one consistent brand.
