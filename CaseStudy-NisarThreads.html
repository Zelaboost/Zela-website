The brief

Take a fabric business online, properly.

Nisar Threads sells premium unstitched fabric for men's shalwar kameez: Boski, Toyobo wash-and-wear, check and silky fabrics. They needed a store where customers anywhere in Pakistan could browse by season, see sale prices clearly and order with confidence.

What we built

Everything a fabric store needs.

Shop by season

Summer, Winter and Four Seasons collections, so customers find the right fabric for the weather.

Sales that sell

Seasonal sale sections with original and discounted prices side by side, plus coupon codes at checkout.

Smooth checkout

Cart with order notes and shipping estimates, customer accounts and secure payment.

Wishlist & search

Customers can save fabrics for later and search the full range in seconds.

Built-in trust

Free shipping over Rs 5,000, 7-day exchanges, 24/7 support and clear shipping, refund and privacy policies.

Customers who come back

Newsletter sign-up and customer accounts, so every sale can lead to the next one.
