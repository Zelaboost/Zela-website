The challenge

Turn a new development into real enquiries.

Paramount City wanted a stronger digital presence, to reach potential property buyers and to generate high-quality leads through social media.

Our strategy

Show the progress, then ask for the lead.

We created engaging content, edited professional promotional videos, managed social media and launched targeted Meta Ads campaigns aimed at likely buyers.

The outcome

Consistent enquiries, at a low cost.

600+ leads in 50 days at an average of PKR 90 each: a cost-effective flow of property enquiries for the sales team.
