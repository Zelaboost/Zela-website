The brief

Luxury you can wear every day, seen by more people.

Silver Collection by M.A sells elegant silver and artificial jewellery, with worldwide shipping. Jewellery sells on detail and sparkle, which photos alone struggle to show. The goal was to put the pieces in motion, in front of far more people, and turn that attention into profile visits, clicks and followers.

We built the account around short, hand-held reels showing each piece up close, backed by Meta ads to push the best performers further.

01

Video editing

Fast, close-up reels that show bangles, rings and sets catching the light, cut to hold attention in the first seconds.

02

Meta ad management

Paid campaigns across Instagram and Facebook to extend reach and drive profile visits and link clicks.

03

Social media handling

Daily posting and account management, with highlights for reviews, offers, exhibitions and in-stock pieces.

04

Graphic design

On-brand visuals for offers, price drops and collection posts that keep the feed consistent.
